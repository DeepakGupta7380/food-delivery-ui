Our mission is to elevate the quality of life of the urban consumer by offering unparalleled convenience. Convenience is what makes us tick. It’s what makes us get out of bed and say, “Let’s do this.”

INDUSTRY PIONEER

Being among the first few entrants, Swiggy has successfully pioneered the hyperlocal commerce industry in India, launching Food Delivery in 2014 and Quick Commerce in 2020.

Due to the pioneering status of Swiggy, it is well-recognised as a leader in innovation in hyperlocal commerce and a category-defining brand; trusted, intuitive and deeply embedded in the daily routines of millions of Indians.
